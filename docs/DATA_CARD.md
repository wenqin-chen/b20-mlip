(manifest `runs/data.pull/20260918T201513-993c10-0/manifest.json`, git `0c14b441`, 51 s) and

### 2.1 MPtrj (label_source `mptrj`, energy_scale `mp`, config_type `relax`)
* Screen: the element set of each member's first frame; members whose set is a subset of
  {Mn, Fe, Co, Si, Ge} with at least two elements are parsed (145,919 members scanned in ~5 s;
  154 in-family materials; 81 elemental members skipped and counted).
* 1,460 frames by element set: Fe-Si 171, Mn-Si 169, Fe-Ge 151, Ge-Mn 137, Co-Fe 114, Co-Ge 114,
  Co-Si 82, Fe-Mn-Si 78, Co-Mn-Si 75, Co-Ge-Mn 68, Fe-Mn 52, Co-Fe-Si 42, Co-Fe-Ge 40, Ge-Si 37,
  Co-Mn 29, Fe-Ge-Mn 25, Fe-Ge-Mn-Si 21, Co-Ge-Si 18, Co-Ge-Mn-Si 15, Ge-Mn-Si 9, Fe-Ge-Si 7,
  Co-Fe-Ge-Mn 6 (matches the ~1,450 / 154 estimate in `docs/design/facts_2026-09-18.md`).
* B20 subset (`mptrj_b20.extxyz`): 8-atom TM-X cells with spglib space group 198 at
  symprec 0.1 — **65 frames** from six materials: FeSi mp-871 (14), MnSi mp-1431 (12), CoSi
  mp-7577 (13), FeGe mp-21255 (8), **CoGe mp-10692 (4)** and **MnGe mp-1078464 (14)**. The last
  two were missed by the earlier by-id extraction `data/raw/mptrj/b20_mptrj.extxyz` (60 frames);
  that file also contains 13 frames of the non-cubic FeGe polymorph mp-22510, which spglib
  assigns to space group 12 (C2/m) at both symprec 0.1 and 0.01, so they are *not* B20 and are
  excluded here (they remain in `mptrj_infamily.extxyz` as `FeGe/mptrj/mp-22510`). Overlap with
  the old file: 47 of its 60 frames. MnGe (SPEC §3 stretch goal) therefore has a B20 parent in
  MPtrj (a = 4.762 Å, Mn moments ≈ 2.15 μB) without an OPTIMADE search.
* Per frame: `energy` (raw MPtrj total energy, eV), `forces` (eV/Å), `magmoms` where the task
  was spin-polarised, `stress=None`. The extxyz export carries a `stress` key of unverified unit
  and sign (the MPtrj JSON stores kBar with the VASP sign); it is preserved verbatim as
  `info["mptrj_stress_raw_voigt6"]` and must never be trained or evaluated on.
  `info` also holds `mp_id`, `task_id`, `calc_id`, `ionic_step`, `formula`,
  `mp2020_corrected_energy`, `mptrj_frame_id` (MPtrj's `<task>-<calc>-<step>` id).
  `frame_id` is the project geometry hash, `group_id="<compound>/mptrj/<mp-id>"`,
  `parent_id=<mp-id>`.
* Role (SPEC §4): seen by MACE-MPA-0 → forgetting tier T4a and replay only; never a zero-shot
  vs fine-tuned test.

### 2.2 OMat24 1M subsplit (label_source `omat24`, energy_scale `omat24`, config_type `omat24`)
* Read natively (`lmdb` + `orjson`; `fairchem-core` cannot coexist with `mace-torch==0.3.16`):
  integer keys → zlib-compressed orjson ase.db rows; arrays appear either as nested lists or as
  ase.db `{"__ndarray__": [shape, dtype, flat]}` objects (both decoded); the `nextid` key is
  skipped. 1,171,309 rows scanned in ~25 s.
* In-family (subset of {Mn, Fe, Co, Si, Ge}, ≥ 2 elements): **656 frames** — identical to the
  earlier extraction `data/raw/omat24/omat24_inchemsys.extxyz` (656). 42 elemental frames
  skipped. Force cap max‖F‖ ≤ 15 eV/Å removes **179** → **477 kept**. spglib 198 at symprec 0.1:
  **0** (rattled/AIMD frames of small cells, 2–13 atoms; no B20 parent can be identified).
* Kept frames by element set: Co-Fe 97, Co-Si 44, Fe-Si 36, Mn-Si 35, Co-Fe-Mn 27, Co-Mn 27,
  Co-Fe-Si 24, Co-Fe-Ge 23, Fe-Mn-Si 22, Fe-Ge 21, Fe-Mn 21, Co-Mn-Si 19, Co-Ge-Si 13,
  Fe-Ge-Mn 13, Co-Ge 12, Ge-Mn 11, Ge-Si 11, Fe-Ge-Si 7, Co-Ge-Mn 6, Ge-Mn-Si 5, Fe-Ge-Mn-Si 3.
  By OMat24 calc id: aimd-from-PBE-1000-npt 144, aimd-from-PBE-1000-nvt 133, rattled-relax 50,
  aimd-from-PBE-3000-npt 35, rattled-1000 35, aimd-from-PBE-3000-nvt 26, rattled-500 17,
  rattled-300 13, rattled-300-subsampled 9, rattled-500-subsampled 9, rattled-1000-subsampled 6
  (train 384, val 39, test 54 by OMat24 split).
* Units: energies eV, forces eV/Å, `stress` = the ase.db Voigt-6 `[xx, yy, zz, yz, xz, xy]` in
  eV/Å^3 passed through unchanged. Convention check (one-off, 22 frames spanning all 11 calc
  ids): stored stress vs MACE-MPA-0 stress (ASE sign, `atoms.get_stress()`) correlates at
  r = +0.965 (r = −0.965 against the flipped sign), medians 0.0162 vs 0.0170 eV/Å^3; stored vs

  (negative trace = compressed cell); no sign flip or unit conversion is applied.
* Lineage: `parent_id` = OMat24 `data["parent_id"]` (the Alexandria parent, e.g.
  `agm002241788_ABC2_3_spg225`), `group_id="<compound>/omat24/<parent_id>"`; `info` keeps

  `omat24_prototype_label`, `omat24_parent_prototype_label`, `omat24_composition_reduced`,
  `omat24_unique_id`, `omat24_shard`, `omat24_row` and `energy_corrected_mp2020` when present.
* The training rule R4 (`config_energy_weight=0`) is applied by the train tier, not here.
  `data pull --delete-raw` removes the shards only after a successful filter (never default).

### 2.3 WBM sample (`data/wbm/sample_1000_s0.json`)
* Protocol: `ids = sorted(material_id)`; `numpy.random.default_rng(0).permutation`; first
  1,000. Stability truth = `e_above_hull_mp2020_corrected_ppd_mp ≤ 0` (MP2020-corrected energy
  above the MP patched-phase-diagram hull, the matbench-discovery column); formation energy =
  `e_form_per_atom_mp2020_corrected`.
* Population: 256,963 entries, 42,825 stable → prevalence **16.67 %** (no missing hull values).
  Sample of 1,000 (seed 0): 152 stable → **15.2 %** (the realised prevalence of this seed;
  `eval discovery` must report it, not the population value).
* In-family stratum (formula elements ⊆ {Mn, Fe, Co, Si, Ge}): **312 entries, 4 stable
  (1.28 %)** — any in-family metric must state N = 312.
* The JSON stores per-id truth (`formula`, `n_sites`, `e_form_per_atom`, `e_above_hull`,
  `stable`) for the 1,000 sampled and the 312 in-family ids, and sha256/bytes of both source
  files. Initial structures are read member-by-member from the zip (`wbm.atoms_for_ids`).

### 2.5 Round-0 candidates (`data sample`, label_source `none`, no labels)
Parents = the most relaxed (smallest max|F|) spg-198 MPtrj frame per compound:
FeSi mp-871, CoSi mp-7577, MnSi mp-1431, FeGe mp-21255, MnGe mp-1078464. Per parent, before
dedupe: 24 strain (±2/4/6 %, isotropic + x/y/z uniaxial), 12 shear (±2/4 % on xy/xz/yz),
30 rattle (σ = 0.05/0.10/0.15 Å × 10 draws, seed 0), 11 EOS volume points (±8 %), 2 vacancies
(one TM, one Si/Ge; 63 atoms in 2×2×2), 5 phonon supercells (phonopy 2×2×2, 0.03 Å: the
undisplaced reference `phonopy_disp_number=-1` + 4 symmetry-reduced displacements; 64 atoms)
→ 84 × 5 = 420. MD snapshots (`--md`, MACE-MPA-0 NVT at 300/600/900 K, 10 per T) are off by
default. `group_id="<compound>/<config_type>/<parent frame_id>"`, `parent_id` = parent
`frame_id`; generator parameters live in `info`.

## 3 Filters (`data filter`)
* **Dedupe**: pymatgen `StructureMatcher(ltol=0.002, stol=0.005, angle_tol=0.5°, scale=False,
  primitive_cell=False, attempt_supercell=False)`, first frame of a cluster kept, only frames
  with identical sorted atomic numbers compared. Because matching is up to rotation, the
  symmetry-equivalent copies a cubic parent produces (x/y/z uniaxial strains; ±γ and xy/xz/yz
  shears) merge — they would cost identical DFT runs and teach an E(3)-equivariant model nothing.
  On the round-0 candidates: 420 → **300** (per parent 12 strain, 2 shear, 9 EOS — the 0.936 and
  1.064 volume points sit within 0.2 % of the ±2 % isotropic strains — 30 rattle, 2 vacancy,
  5 phonon).
* **Force cap**: frames with forces and max‖F‖ > `cfg.data.force_cap_eVA` (15 eV/Å) are dropped;
  unlabelled frames pass.
* **Magnetic branch** (QE frames): reject `converged=False`, and reject
  `|m_per_TM − m_ref[compound]| > cfg.dft.branch_tol_muB` (0.3 μB) with
  `m_per_TM = abs_magnetization / n_TM` (QE "absolute magnetization"; `|total_magnetization| /
  n_TM` if the absolute value is absent). Frames without a transition metal, without a reference
  entry (`--m-ref JSON` or `cfg.dft.m_ref_muB`) or without any magnetization value are kept and
  counted; the manifest records every rejection with its reason and moment. Kept `DFTFrame`s get
  `branch_ok=True`; plain frames get `info["branch_ok"]=True`.

## 4 Splits (`data split`, policy `group_hash`)
`u = int(sha256("<group_id>|<seed>")[:16], 16) / 2^64`; groups with `u < 0.8` train, `< 0.9` val,
else test. Only `label_source="qe"` frames can be trained on (no mixed energy scales, SPEC §2).
Exclusions move frames to test: T1 `temperature_K > 600` (frame-level), T2 compounds FeGe/MnGe,
T3 all OMat24, T4a all MPtrj. Tiers: T0 = val+test QE frames, T1–T4a as above (every matching
frame), T4b = the WBM sample ids. `split_id = sha256(frames_sha256, seed, fractions, policy,
holdout, max_train_T, train_sources)[:12]`; the file is `data/splits/<split_id>.json`.
